ADVERTISING TECHNOLOGY

Tools built for our own publishing operation.

Creative Journey develops internal software and workflows to help us understand, manage, and improve advertising for the books we publish.

BUSINESS PURPOSE

Direct advertiser use.

Our advertising technology supports Creative Journey's own publishing business and advertiser accounts. It is intended to reduce repetitive manual work and provide better visibility into campaign performance.

We are not currently offering this technology as a third-party advertising service, agency platform, or commercial software product for outside advertisers.

INTERNAL CAPABILITIES

Operational tools for better decisions.

01

Reporting & Analysis

Consolidating campaign results so Creative Journey can evaluate advertising performance across its own catalog.

02

Keyword & Bid Review

Supporting analysis of search terms, keywords, bids, and efficiency to inform advertising decisions for our own titles.

03

Budget Management

Helping monitor spend and campaign budgets so advertising activity can be managed consistently and responsibly.

DATA USE & ACCESS

Limited to the purpose for which access is granted.

Any access to a third-party advertising platform is subject to that platform's approval, authorization, permissions, agreements, and applicable policies.

If API or other platform access is granted, Creative Journey intends to use authorized advertising data only to operate, analyze, and optimize its own advertising activity and related internal business workflows.

We do not represent that Creative Journey has received API access, certification, partner status, or platform approval unless that authorization has actually been granted.

FOR PLATFORM REVIEWERS

Creative Journey Press is the advertiser and publisher.

The requested use case is internal advertising management for Creative Journey's own published products. The technology is being developed to support our direct advertiser operations rather than to provide advertising services to third parties.
